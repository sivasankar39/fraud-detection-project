Understanding Model Performance

Precision

Of all transactions predicted as fraud, precision measures the proportion that are actually fraudulent.

Recall

Of all actual fraudulent transactions, recall measures how many the model successfully detects.

Average Precision

Summarizes the Precision–Recall curve using a stepwise weighted average of precision across recall changes.

Business Impact

Higher precision reduces unnecessary fraud alerts, while higher recall reduces missed fraudulent transactions.
